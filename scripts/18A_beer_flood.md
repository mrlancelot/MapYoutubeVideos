## Narration

A tsunami of beer killed 8 people. In 1814, a brewery vat exploded in London. 135,000 gallons of beer flooded the streets. The wave destroyed two homes. Collapsed a pub. People drowned in beer. One woman died at a wake for someone else. The brewery wasn't even fined. They got a tax refund for the lost beer. Victorian priorities. Eight people dead from alcohol. Without even drinking it.
