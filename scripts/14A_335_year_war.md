# The War That Lasted 335 Years With Zero Deaths
**Duration:** 30 seconds | **Words:** 116 | **WPS:** 4.0

**FORMAT:** Optimized A-Series (Shock Hook + Loop Ending)

## Narration

A war lasted 335 years. Zero casualties. In 1651, the Netherlands declared war on the Isles of Scilly. Tiny islands off England. Then they just... forgot. For 335 years. No battles. No invasions. Nothing. In 1986, a historian noticed they were still technically at war. Both sides signed a peace treaty. The Dutch ambassador apologized for the "stress" of being at war. The islanders laughed. Nobody had noticed for three centuries.
