## Narration

A pig almost started a war between America and Britain. In 1859, an American farmer shot a British pig eating his potatoes. Britain demanded payment. America refused. Both countries sent warships. Soldiers aimed cannons at each other. For a pig. For 12 years, they had a standoff. No shots fired. Just... waiting. Finally, a German mediator said America gets the island. The most peaceful war in history. All because one pig ate some potatoes.
