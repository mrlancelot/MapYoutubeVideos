# How Did America Almost Nuke North Carolina?
**Duration:** 40 seconds | **Style:** Reference Match

---

## Narration

How did America almost nuke North Carolina? In January 1961, a B-52 bomber broke apart over Goldsboro. Two hydrogen bombs fell from the sky. Each bomb was two hundred and fifty times more powerful than Hiroshima. One bomb's parachute deployed and it landed intact. The other slammed into farmland at seven hundred miles per hour. When the military recovered it, they discovered that three of the four safety switches had failed. One switch. One single switch had kept a four-megaton nuclear bomb from detonating on American soil. The blast would have killed everyone within eight miles. Radioactive fallout would have reached New York, Philadelphia, and Washington DC. The government classified this incident for decades. Sometimes the greatest threat to America came from America itself.
