# Why Did Japan Close Its Doors for 220 Years?
**Duration:** 40 seconds | **Style:** Reference Match

---

## Narration

Why did Japan disappear from the world for two hundred and twenty years? In 1633, the Tokugawa shogunate watched European colonizers conquering Asia. The Philippines had fallen. Indonesia was next. Christianity was spreading. Guns were changing warfare. So Japan made a radical decision. They closed everything. No foreigner could enter. No Japanese could leave. The punishment for trying was death. For over two centuries, Japan existed in almost complete isolation. One small Dutch trading post was the only Western contact allowed. When American warships finally forced Japan open in 1853, they found a nation frozen in time. But within fifty years, Japan transformed itself into a world power that defeated Russia. Isolation had preserved them. Opening them transformed them into an empire.
