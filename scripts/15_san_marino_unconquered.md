# How Has San Marino Survived 1,700 Years Unconquered?
**Duration:** 40 seconds | **Style:** Reference Match

---

## Narration

How has a microstate survived one thousand seven hundred years without being conquered? San Marino. Twenty-four square miles completely surrounded by Italy. It was founded in 301 AD when a Christian stonemason fled Roman persecution and established a community on top of Mount Titano. Somehow, every empire that rose and fell around it left this tiny republic alone. The Roman Empire collapsed. San Marino survived. Napoleon conquered all of Europe. He was so impressed by their republic that he offered to expand their territory. They politely declined. Garibaldi unified Italy. San Marino remained separate. World War I and II devastated the continent. San Marino stayed neutral. Today, it remains the oldest surviving republic in the world. Its survival strategy was simple. Be too small to bother with, too high to easily conquer, and too wise to ever take sides.
