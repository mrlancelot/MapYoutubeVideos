# How Did Hitler Plan to Attack the United States?
**Duration:** 35 seconds | **Words:** 130 | **WPS:** 3.7

**FORMAT:** Viral Multi-Country (Secret Plans)

---

## Narration

Hitler had plans to bomb New York City. Amerika Bomber. A plane that could fly from Germany to America and back. 11,000 kilometer range. Targets: Manhattan. Washington DC. American factories. The Nazis even designed rockets to hit the US from submarines. The A9/A10. An intercontinental ballistic missile. In 1942. They never built enough. The war ended too soon. But the scientists who designed these weapons were captured by America. Operation Paperclip. Nazi rocket scientists built America's space program. The plans to bomb America became the plans to reach the moon.
