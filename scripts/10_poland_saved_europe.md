# How Did Poland Save Europe and Then Get Erased from the Map?
**Duration:** 40 seconds | **Style:** Reference Match

---

## Narration

How did Poland save Europe and then get erased from the map? In 1683, the Ottoman Empire besieged Vienna with an army of one hundred and fifty thousand soldiers. If Vienna fell, all of Central Europe would be open to conquest. The Holy Roman Emperor begged Poland for help. King Jan Sobieski answered. He led the largest cavalry charge in history, eighteen thousand winged hussars thundering down the hillside. The Ottoman army was shattered. Europe was saved. But Poland's neighbors did not return the favor. Over the next century, Russia, Prussia, and Austria carved up Polish territory piece by piece. By 1795, Poland had completely vanished from the map. The nation that had saved Europe ceased to exist. It would take one hundred and twenty-three years before Poland appeared on a map again.
