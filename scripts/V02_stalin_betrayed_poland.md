# Why Did Stalin Betray His Own Allies During the Invasion of Poland?
**Duration:** 35 seconds | **Words:** 128 | **WPS:** 3.7

**FORMAT:** Viral Multi-Country (Betrayal + WW2)

---

## Narration

In 1939, Hitler invaded Poland from the west. Two weeks later, Stalin invaded from the east. They had a secret deal. The Molotov-Ribbentrop Pact. Nazi Germany and Soviet Russia agreed to split Poland between them. Britain and France declared war on Germany. But not on Russia. Even though both invaded the same country. Stalin got half of Poland without consequences. Two years later, Hitler betrayed Stalin and invaded Russia. The man who betrayed Poland got betrayed himself. Karma on a global scale.
