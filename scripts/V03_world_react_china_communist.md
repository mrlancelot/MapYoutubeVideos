# How Did the World React When China Turned Communist?
**Duration:** 35 seconds | **Words:** 125 | **WPS:** 3.6

**FORMAT:** Viral Multi-Country (World Reaction)

---

## Narration

In 1949, Mao won. China turned communist. The world panicked. America had just won World War 2. Now they lost the most populated country on Earth to communism. The domino theory was born. If China fell, all of Asia would follow. Korea. Vietnam. Thailand. America poured billions into stopping the spread. The Korean War started one year later. The Vietnam War after that. One country turning communist led to decades of American wars in Asia. China's revolution changed everything.
