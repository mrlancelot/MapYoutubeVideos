# Why Did Britain Go to War with China Over Tea?
**Duration:** 40 seconds | **Style:** Reference Match

---

## Narration

Why did Britain go to war with China over tea? In the early 1800s, Britain had a serious problem. They were addicted to Chinese tea, but China would only accept silver as payment. Britain was bleeding money. So they came up with a solution. They would sell opium grown in India to Chinese merchants, creating millions of addicts. When the Qing Emperor tried to stop the trade and destroyed twenty thousand chests of opium, Britain responded with warships. In two devastating wars, they crushed China's military. Hong Kong was seized. Shanghai was forced open. And in the ultimate humiliation, China had to pay for the opium they had tried to destroy. This began what the Chinese call their Century of Humiliation, a wound that still shapes their worldview today.
