# How Did Liechtenstein Leave for War with 80 Soldiers and Return with 81?
**Duration:** 40 seconds | **Style:** Reference Match

---

## Narration

How did Liechtenstein leave for war with eighty soldiers and return with eighty-one? In 1866, the tiny principality of Liechtenstein was forced to send troops to the Austro-Prussian War. They sent their entire army. Eighty men. Their mission was to guard a mountain pass in northern Italy. But by the time they arrived, the war was already ending. They never saw combat. They never fired a single shot. When the army marched home, they brought someone with them. An Austrian liaison officer who had become their friend during the campaign. He decided to return with them rather than go back to Austria alone. Liechtenstein had sent eighty soldiers to war and returned with eighty-one. It remains the only country in history to gain a soldier in a war without fighting.
