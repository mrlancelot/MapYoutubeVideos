# How Did Austria Lose a Battle Against Itself?
**Duration:** 40 seconds | **Style:** Reference Match

---

## Narration

How did Austria lose a battle against itself? In 1788, the Austrian Empire was at war with the Ottoman Turks. Near the town of Karansebes, Austrian scouts encountered a group of Romani people selling alcohol. The cavalry bought the liquor but refused to share with the infantry. A fight broke out between the two groups. Someone fired a shot. In the darkness and confusion, some soldiers began shouting that the Turks were attacking. Panic spread through the camp like wildfire. Austrian units began firing at each other, each believing the other was the enemy. By morning, ten thousand Austrian soldiers lay dead or wounded. The Ottoman army was still two days away. When they finally arrived, they found an empty battlefield and easily captured the town. Austria had defeated itself.
