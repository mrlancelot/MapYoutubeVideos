## Narration

Rome didn't fall to barbarians. Rome poisoned itself. For centuries, Romans drank water from lead pipes. They ate from lead dishes. They even sweetened their wine with lead syrup. It tasted sweet. It was killing them. Their emperors went insane. Caligula. Nero. The madness wasn't a coincidence. Their birth rates collapsed. Fewer Romans were being born. The empire that conquered the known world was slowly dying from the inside. And here's the proof: scientists found massive amounts of lead in Roman bones. The pipes that built the greatest civilization in history destroyed it. Rome wasn't conquered. It poisoned itself to death.
