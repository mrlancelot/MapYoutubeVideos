# How a Belgian King Became a Hero by Losing Almost His Entire Country
**Duration:** 35 seconds | **Words:** 128 | **WPS:** 3.7

**FORMAT:** Viral Multi-Country (Underdog Hero)

---

## Narration

Germany invaded Belgium in 1914. Within weeks, they controlled 95% of the country. King Albert I refused to surrender. He retreated to a tiny corner behind the Yser River. And held it. For four years. The Belgian army flooded the fields, creating an impassable swamp. Germany threw everything at them. They didn't break. King Albert fought alongside his soldiers. His wife served as a nurse. His son fought in the trenches. By 1918, Belgium was liberated. A king who lost almost everything became the most beloved monarch in Belgian history.
