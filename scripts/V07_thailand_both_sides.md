## Narration

Thailand officially allied with Japan in World War 2. They declared war on America and Britain. But secretly, the Thai ambassador in Washington refused to deliver the declaration. He started a resistance movement instead. Thai soldiers fought for Japan on the surface. Underground, they helped Allied spies and rescued prisoners. When Japan lost, Thailand said they were forced into it. The secret resistance proved it. Thailand switched sides without anyone noticing. The only country to fight for both sides and get away with it.
