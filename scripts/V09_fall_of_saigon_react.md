# How Did the World React to the Fall of Saigon?
**Duration:** 35 seconds | **Words:** 128 | **WPS:** 3.7

**FORMAT:** Viral Multi-Country (World Reaction)

## Narration

April 30, 1975. Saigon fell. The Vietnam War was over. America had lost. Helicopters evacuated the last Americans from rooftops. Thousands of Vietnamese tried to escape. The world watched in shock. America, the superpower, defeated by a small communist nation. The Soviet Union celebrated. China celebrated. American allies in Asia panicked. Would America abandon them too? South Korea. Taiwan. Japan. They all questioned American commitment. One city falling made the entire world doubt American power.
