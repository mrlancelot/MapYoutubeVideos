# How Did the World React When China Invaded Tibet?
**Duration:** 35 seconds | **Words:** 128 | **WPS:** 3.7

**FORMAT:** Viral Multi-Country (World Reaction)

---

## Narration

In 1950, China invaded Tibet. 40,000 Chinese soldiers against 8,500 Tibetan troops. It was over in weeks. The Dalai Lama was 15 years old. The world did nothing. America was busy with Korea. Britain didn't want to upset China. India didn't want a war on their border. The UN debated but took no action. Tibet appealed to the world. Nobody came. By 1959, the Dalai Lama fled to India. He's still there. A country erased while the world watched and did nothing.
