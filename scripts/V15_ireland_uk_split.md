# Why is One Part of Ireland Still Part of the United Kingdom?

## Narration

Ireland fought Britain for centuries. In 1921, they finally got independence. But not all of it. Six counties in the north stayed British. Why? Protestant settlers. For 400 years, Britain sent Protestants to Ireland. In the north, they became the majority. When Ireland voted for independence, Northern Ireland voted to stay. The island was partitioned. This caused decades of conflict. The Troubles. Catholics vs Protestants. IRA bombings. British soldiers on Irish streets. 3,500 people died. One partition. One island split by religion and history. Still divided today.
