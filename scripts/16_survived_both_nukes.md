# How Did One Man Survive Both Atomic Bombs?
**Duration:** 40 seconds | **Style:** Reference Match

---

## Narration

How did one man survive both atomic bombs? On August 6th, 1945, Tsutomu Yamaguchi was in Hiroshima on a business trip. He was two miles from ground zero when the bomb dropped. His eardrums ruptured. Burns covered his body. But he survived. Three days later, he dragged himself back to his home city to recover. That city was Nagasaki. On August 9th, he was explaining the Hiroshima explosion to his boss when the second atomic bomb fell on Nagasaki. The same man. Two nuclear explosions. Three days apart. He survived again. Tsutomu Yamaguchi lived to ninety-three years old. He remains the only person officially recognized by Japan as having survived both atomic bombs.
