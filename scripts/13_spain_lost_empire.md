# How Did Spain Lose a 400-Year Empire in Four Months?
**Duration:** 40 seconds | **Style:** Reference Match

---

## Narration

How did Spain lose a four hundred year empire in just four months? By 1898, Spain still controlled Cuba, Puerto Rico, the Philippines, and Guam. They had held these territories for centuries, surviving countless rebellions and independence movements. Then the United States declared war. The Spanish navy was obsolete. Their ships were relics from another era. In the Philippines, the American fleet destroyed the entire Spanish squadron without losing a single man. In Cuba, the story was the same. By August, Spain had lost every overseas colony except a few outposts in Africa. The nation that had conquered the Americas in the sixteenth century was expelled from them entirely in the nineteenth. Four hundred years of empire, erased in one hundred and thirteen days.
