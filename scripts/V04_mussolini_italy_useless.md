# Why Was Mussolini's Italy So Useless in World War 2?
**Duration:** 35 seconds | **Words:** 130 | **WPS:** 3.7

**FORMAT:** Viral Multi-Country (Military Failure)

---

## Narration

Italy was supposed to be a great power. They weren't. Mussolini invaded Greece. Got pushed back. Germany had to rescue them. Invaded North Africa. Got destroyed. Germany had to rescue them. Invaded France. After France had already surrendered to Germany. Still struggled. Italian soldiers had outdated weapons. Terrible supply lines. Officers who didn't care. When the Allies invaded Sicily, Italy surrendered in 43 days. Mussolini was so bad, Hitler had to fight on two extra fronts just to save his useless ally.
