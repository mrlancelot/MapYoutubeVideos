# How Did a Wrong Turn Start World War I?
**Duration:** 40 seconds | **Style:** Reference Match

---

## Narration

How did a wrong turn start World War I? On June 28th, 1914, Archduke Franz Ferdinand visited Sarajevo in Bosnia. Serbian nationalists had planned his assassination. One threw a bomb at his car, but it bounced off and exploded behind them. The attack had failed. Later that day, the Archduke's driver took a wrong turn and stalled the car. By pure coincidence, they had stopped right in front of a cafe where one of the failed assassins, Gavrilo Princip, was eating lunch. He couldn't believe his luck. He walked up to the car and fired two shots, killing the Archduke and his wife. Austria blamed Serbia. Russia mobilized to defend Serbia. Germany backed Austria. Within six weeks, all the great powers of Europe were at war. Twenty million people would die, all because one driver made a wrong turn.
