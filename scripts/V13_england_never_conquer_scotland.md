# Why England Could Never Conquer Scotland
**Duration:** 35 seconds | **Words:** 128 | **WPS:** 3.7

**FORMAT:** Viral Multi-Country (Historical Rivalry)

---

## Narration

England tried to conquer Scotland for 400 years. They failed. Edward I nearly succeeded in 1296. Then William Wallace rebelled. Braveheart. Robert the Bruce won independence at Bannockburn in 1314. England tried again. And again. Scotland allied with France, England's enemy. The Auld Alliance. Every time England attacked Scotland, France attacked England. For centuries, tiny Scotland survived against a much larger neighbor. In the end, Scotland wasn't conquered. A Scottish king inherited the English throne in 1603. Scotland technically won.
