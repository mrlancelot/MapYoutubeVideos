# How Did Iceland Beat Britain Four Times Without an Army?
**Duration:** 40 seconds | **Style:** Reference Match

---

## Narration

How did Iceland beat Britain four times without an army? Between 1958 and 1976, Iceland and Britain fought the Cod Wars. Iceland's fishing waters were the heart of its economy, but British trawlers were depleting the fish stocks. Iceland had no navy. Britain had one of the most powerful fleets in the world. But Iceland had leverage. During the Cold War, Iceland sat right in the middle of the NATO alliance's most strategic location in the North Atlantic. Every time Britain sent warships, Iceland threatened to leave NATO. The United States couldn't afford to lose Iceland's radar stations that tracked Soviet submarines. Four times, Britain was forced to back down. The tiny nation of three hundred thousand people had beaten a former superpower using nothing but geography and clever diplomacy.
