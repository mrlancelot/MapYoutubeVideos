# How Did a War Last 335 Years Without a Single Battle?
**Duration:** 40 seconds | **Style:** Reference Match

---

## Narration

How did a war last three hundred and thirty-five years without a single battle? In 1651, during the English Civil War, the Royalist navy fled to the Isles of Scilly off the coast of Cornwall. The Netherlands, allied with Parliament, sent an admiral to demand reparations for Dutch ships the Royalists had attacked. When the Royalists refused, the Dutch declared war on the Isles of Scilly specifically. But before any fighting began, the Royalists surrendered to Parliamentary forces and left the islands. The Dutch sailed home. Everyone forgot about the declaration of war. For over three centuries, the Netherlands and the Isles of Scilly remained technically at war. No shots were ever fired. No battles were ever fought. In 1986, a historian discovered the forgotten conflict. A peace treaty was finally signed, ending the longest and most peaceful war in history.
