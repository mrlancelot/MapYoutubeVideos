# How Did a Wall Split Europe for 28 Years?
**Duration:** 40 seconds | **Style:** Reference Match

---

## Narration

How did a wall split families for twenty-eight years? In 1961, East German soldiers began laying barbed wire through Berlin in the middle of the night. By morning, a city was divided. Families woke up separated. People who lived on one side worked on the other. Many never saw each other again. The wall grew into concrete fortifications with guard towers and shoot-to-kill orders. At least one hundred and forty people died trying to cross it. For twenty-eight years, this wall stood as the symbol of the Cold War. Then on November 9th, 1989, a confused East German spokesman accidentally announced that the border was open. Thousands rushed the gates. The guards did not shoot. Within hours, the wall was falling. A twenty-eight year divide ended because of one mistake at a press conference.
