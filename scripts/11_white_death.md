# How Did One Man Kill 500 Soldiers in 100 Days?
**Duration:** 40 seconds | **Style:** Reference Match

---

## Narration

How did one man kill five hundred soldiers in one hundred days? In 1939, the Soviet Union invaded Finland with over eight hundred thousand troops. Finland had only three hundred and forty thousand. They were outnumbered and outgunned. But they had Simo Häyhä. A Finnish farmer turned sniper who hunted Soviet soldiers in temperatures reaching minus forty degrees. He used an iron-sighted rifle with no scope to avoid glare that might give away his position. In just one hundred days, he recorded five hundred confirmed kills. The Soviets called him the White Death. They sent counter-snipers. They called in artillery strikes. Nothing worked. Finally, a Soviet soldier shot him in the face with an explosive bullet. He survived. He woke up from a coma the day the war ended. Simo Häyhä lived to ninety-six, the deadliest sniper in recorded history.
