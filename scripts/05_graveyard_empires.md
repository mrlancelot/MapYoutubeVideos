# Why Is Afghanistan Called the Graveyard of Empires?
**Duration:** 40 seconds | **Style:** Reference Match

---

## Narration

Why is Afghanistan called the Graveyard of Empires? Alexander the Great conquered everything from Greece to India, but Afghanistan bled his army for three years. He never fully controlled it. In 1842, the British Empire sent sixteen thousand soldiers into Afghanistan. One came back. The Soviet Union invaded in 1979 with overwhelming force. Ten years later, they withdrew in defeat. Their empire collapsed two years later. Then came the United States. Twenty years and two trillion dollars later, they left in 2021, with the Taliban back in control, exactly where they started. Every superpower has tried to conquer this mountainous land. Every superpower has failed. The terrain is unforgiving. The tribes never surrender. For over two thousand years, Afghanistan has earned its name as the Graveyard of Empires.
