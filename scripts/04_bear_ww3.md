# How Did a Bear Almost Start World War III?
**Duration:** 40 seconds | **Style:** Reference Match

---

## Narration

How did a bear almost start World War III? It was October 1962, the height of the Cuban Missile Crisis. American and Soviet forces were on maximum alert, one mistake away from nuclear war. At an air base in Minnesota, a guard spotted something climbing the perimeter fence. Assuming it was a Soviet saboteur, he triggered the intruder alarm. Across the base, nuclear-armed bombers began starting their engines. Pilots climbed into their cockpits, ready to strike the Soviet Union. Then someone made a phone call. The intruder wasn't a Soviet agent. It was a black bear looking for food. The planes were immediately recalled. The world had come within minutes of nuclear war because of one hungry animal that wandered into the wrong place at the worst possible time.
