## Narration

In 1518, people danced themselves to death. It started with one woman in Strasbourg. She danced in the street for days. Then others joined. Then hundreds. 400 people dancing non-stop. They couldn't stop. Their feet bled. Hearts gave out. People died. Authorities thought more dancing would cure it. They hired musicians. Built a stage. More died. Nobody knows why it happened. Mass hysteria? Poisoning? We still don't know. They just kept dancing.
