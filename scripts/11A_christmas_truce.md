## Narration

In the middle of World War I, enemies stopped killing each other. To play soccer. Christmas 1914. German soldiers started singing carols. British soldiers joined in. They climbed out of trenches. Shook hands. Exchanged gifts. Then played football in no-man's land. For one day, the war stopped. Generals were furious. They banned it. Ordered artillery strikes on future Christmas Eves. Because peace was too dangerous. The soldiers wanted to stop. The leaders wouldn't let them.
