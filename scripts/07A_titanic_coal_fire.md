## Narration

The Titanic was on fire before it hit the iceberg. A coal fire had been burning in the hull for weeks. Engineers knew about it. They sailed anyway. The fire weakened the steel. When the iceberg hit, that weakened section ripped open. The "unsinkable" ship was already compromised. Photos show a dark mark on the hull - the burn scar. They covered it up for 100 years. The iceberg didn't sink the Titanic. A fire did.
