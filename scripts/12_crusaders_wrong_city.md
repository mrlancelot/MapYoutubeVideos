# How Did the Crusaders Destroy the Wrong City?
**Duration:** 40 seconds | **Style:** Reference Match

---

## Narration

How did Christian Crusaders destroy the largest Christian city in the world? In 1202, the Fourth Crusade set out to reclaim Jerusalem from Muslim control. But the Crusaders owed Venice a fortune for their ships. Venice had a grudge against Constantinople, the capital of the Byzantine Empire. Their fellow Christians. Venice convinced the Crusaders to attack Constantinople instead. For three days, they looted and burned the greatest Christian city on Earth. They melted church bells. They stole holy relics. They slaughtered thousands of civilians who shared their faith. The Byzantine Empire never fully recovered from the devastation. Two hundred and fifty years later, the Ottomans captured Constantinople. The city that was supposed to protect Christendom had been fatally weakened by Christians themselves.
