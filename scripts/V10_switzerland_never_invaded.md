# Why Was Switzerland Never Invaded by Germany in WW2?
**Duration:** 35 seconds | **Words:** 130 | **WPS:** 3.7

**FORMAT:** Viral Multi-Country (Strategic Mystery)

---

## Narration

Hitler conquered all of Europe. Except Switzerland. Why? It wasn't neutrality. Belgium was neutral too. Germany invaded anyway. Switzerland had three things. First, the Alps. Natural fortress. Second, every citizen was armed and trained. Invasion would be a nightmare. Third, Swiss banks held Nazi gold. Germany needed Switzerland as a financial middleman. Hitler had invasion plans called Operation Tannenbaum. He never used them. Switzerland was more useful alive than conquered. They survived by being too expensive to invade and too useful to destroy.
