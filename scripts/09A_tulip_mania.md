## Narration

A single tulip once cost more than a house. In 1637 Netherlands, tulip bulbs became the most valuable things on Earth. One bulb sold for 10,000 guilders. That's a mansion. A ship. Lifetime wages. People traded everything they owned for flowers. Then in February, someone didn't show up to an auction. Panic. Prices crashed 99% in days. The first financial bubble in history. All because of flowers. People literally went bankrupt over tulips.
