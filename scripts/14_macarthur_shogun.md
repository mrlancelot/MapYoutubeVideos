# How Did an American General Become Japan's Dictator?
**Duration:** 40 seconds | **Style:** Reference Match

---

## Narration

How did an American general become the dictator of Japan? After World War II ended, the Allies planned to divide Japan into occupation zones, just as they had done with Germany. The Soviets wanted Hokkaido. The British and Chinese wanted their share. But General Douglas MacArthur moved faster than anyone expected. As Supreme Commander, he ensured American troops were the only ones on the ground when Japan surrendered. He locked the other Allies out completely. Stalin prepared to invade Hokkaido anyway. At the last moment, he backed down. For the next six years, MacArthur ruled Japan with absolute power. He disarmed millions of soldiers. He rewrote their constitution in a single week. He broke apart the aristocracy and gave women the right to vote. MacArthur had become Japan's new shogun.
