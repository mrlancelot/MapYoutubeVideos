# VIDEO 3: "How Europe Divided Africa Without Asking Africa"
**Duration:** 55 seconds | **Topic:** Berlin Conference / Scramble for Africa

## Narration Script

In 1884, European powers sat in a room in Berlin and carved up an entire continent. No African was invited. Before this, Europeans controlled just 10% of Africa, mostly coastal trading posts. But new technology, steam ships and machine guns, changed everything. Britain wanted it all. So did France. And Germany. And Belgium's King Leopold wanted the Congo, personally. So to avoid war with each other, they made rules. Any European power could claim African land if they occupied it. The result? A feeding frenzy. They drew borders with rulers, straight lines that cut through tribes, through kingdoms, through families. By 1914, only Ethiopia remained free. These arbitrary borders still exist today, the cause of conflicts that never seem to end.

# Script Style Notes

Based on analysis of reference videos:

## Voice/Tone
- Documentary narrator style
- Confident, authoritative
- Fast pacing (~3 words/second)
- No filler words

## Sentence Structure
- Short, punchy sentences
- Power verbs: "seized", "crushed", "shattered", "carved", "bled"
- Dramatic pauses before reveals
- Questions as hooks

## Narrative Techniques
- "But then..." for plot twists
- Specific numbers and dates
- Ironic observations
- Callback to opening at the end

## Visual Integration
- Memes are VISUAL ONLY (not mentioned in narration)
- Memes appear 0.2s after the moment they react to
- Emojis/memes hold 1-2 seconds max
- Let the map tell the story

## Timing Formula
```
Hook question: 0-5 seconds
Setup: 5-20 seconds
Conflict/Action: 20-40 seconds
Resolution/Twist: 40-end
```
